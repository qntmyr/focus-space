Write without the noise.

A split-screen workspace for ADHD brains: your text on the left, a journal on the right to park every distracting thought, a focus timer and ambient sounds. Nothing to sign up for, nothing leaves your device.

Start writing, it's free Opens in your browser. Install it as an app if you like.

Built around one idea: a thought that interrupts you should take two seconds to put away.

Park a distraction

Press B and a timestamped line lands in the journal. Your idea is safe, your text is still there, you keep going.

Focus timer

15 to 50 minute focus blocks with short and long breaks, a gentle chime and a count of today's sessions.

Ambient sounds

Rain, brown noise, wind, café, fireplace, generated in your browser. Mix them, or play the Lofi Girl stream.

A real editor

Headings, lists, checklists, highlights, links and code. Export to PDF, Word, Markdown or plain text.

Zen mode

Press Z and everything disappears except your words. Light, dark or system theme.

Yours, offline

Everything is saved on your device. No account, no tracking of what you write, no server to go down.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The editor, the journal, the timer, the sounds and the exports are free. No trial, no card.

Where is my text stored?

In your browser's local storage, on your device only. Clearing your browser data erases it, so export what matters.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and write.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the app is cached and works without a connection. Only the lo-fi stream needs internet.

Who makes it?

Quentin Mayer, a freelance developer who needed a quieter place to write. mayerquentin.com
